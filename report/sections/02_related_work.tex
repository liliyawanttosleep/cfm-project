\section{Related work}
\textbf{Diffusion models} learn the score of noised data \citep{song2019ncsn,ho2020ddpm,song2021sde}; DDIM \citep{song2021ddim} samples deterministically in few steps, and EDM \citep{karras2022edm} tunes solvers and time grids (FID 1.97 on CIFAR-10 with 35 NFE); \citet{luo2022understanding} unifies their derivations. \textbf{Flow matching} \citep{lipman2023fm,lipman2024guide}, rectified flow \citep{liu2023rf} and stochastic interpolants \citep{albergo2023si} regress a velocity on simple conditional paths and sample with an ODE solver \citep{chen2018node}; OT-CFM \citep{tong2024otcfm} pairs samples by minibatch OT, and Stable Diffusion~3 \citep{esser2024sd3} trains with logit-normal times on shifted grids. \textbf{Few-step generation:} reflow \citep{liu2023rf} retrains on the model's own pairs (one-step FID 378 to 12.2 on CIFAR-10); consistency models \citep{song2023cm} and MeanFlow \citep{geng2025meanflow} learn one-step maps with other objectives. \textbf{Evaluation:} FID \citep{heusel2017fid} is biased upward at finite sample size \citep{chong2020fid} and sensitive to image processing \citep{parmar2022cleanfid}.
