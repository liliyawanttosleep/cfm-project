\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
On MNIST the cheapest large reduction in NFE comes from the sampler and the training-time distribution, not from the coupling, and each finding has a reason derived in Section~\ref{sec:theory}. \textbf{RQ1:} training seeds move FID ten to a hundred times more than sampling noise, so separately trained models need differences above $2\hat\sigma_{\text{seed}}$, while samplers on one model can be compared to a fraction of a point; the $k$-means coverage test detects class imbalance that FID misses, most clearly for DDIM, whose best-FID seed is the least balanced. \textbf{RQ2:} the $\sqrt\kappa$ grid and second-order solvers that avoid the network at $t=1$ cut the steps needed for FID 20 by 28--58\% at no training cost; Heun and RK4 fail on every model we trained, including the nearly straight reflow models, and grids chosen by ODE endpoint error make FID worse. \textbf{RQ3:} minibatch OT is limited by $2\operatorname{tr}\Sigma^{1/2}$, and larger OT batches give no detectable gain at up to twice the cost; logit-normal training times matched the best sampler's NFE@20 on one seed; reflow is the only change that makes one- and two-step sampling usable, because it replaces the random coupling by a deterministic one, but it costs 2.7--2.8 times the training time, lowers the many-step ceiling and erases the difference between I-CFM and OT-CFM. \textbf{RQ4:} reflow is the choice for one to four steps, a better sampler or training-time distribution from about seven steps on; straightness separates groups of models but does not rank models within a group.

\paragraph{Limitations and future work.} The models are small and trained for 50 epochs without EMA, which makes the last-epoch weights noisy and the seed thresholds large. Each variant has one training seed; in particular the logit-normal result rests on a single seed. FID uses ImageNet features, the DDIM reference has two seeds, all experiments use MNIST, and throttled wall times are accurate only to tens of percent. We did not run the $t=1$ diagnostic on the reflow models or separate reflow's ceiling from the effect of fine-tuning. Natural next steps are EMA weights, more seeds for the logit-normal model, combining it with the improved samplers, and a larger pretrained CFM model, e.g.\ on CIFAR-10.
