Flow Matching vs. Diffusion · 17 · Covers the course requirements: linear, tree/SVM, clustering, dimension reduction

[Wentao · ~1.5 min] We use every course method as an evaluation tool, not as an add-on. Each model generates 60,000 MNIST images; our MNIST classifier labels them. Then four analyses. Linear models — logistic regression and LDA — for TSTR: train on synthetic, test on real, and compare with training on real. A small gap means useful synthetic data. Trees and SVM — random forest and RBF-SVM — the same TSTR, plus a real-versus-fake classifier: an AUC near 0.5 means the two sets are hard to tell apart. This is the classifier two-sample test of Lopez-Paz and Oquab. Dimension reduction — PCA, t-SNE and UMAP — put real and generated images in one embedding and compare PCA explained-variance curves. Clustering — k-means with ten clusters and a GMM — ARI and NMI against the labels, and a chi-square test that cluster shares are uniform, which detects mode collapse.
