[Wentao · opening] Hi everyone, I'm Wentao Yang, and this is Kaiming Zhang. Our project compares the two families of generative models behind today's image and video generators: flow matching and diffusion. Today is a proposal, so the focus is on methods and experimental design; at the end we show a 2D experiment that is already finished and what we expect in high dimensions.
