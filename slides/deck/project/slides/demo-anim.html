Flow Matching vs. Diffusion · 18

[Wentao · ~1.5 min] Now our results. This animation shows the three trained models generating two-moons samples from the same 1,500 starting points, 60 steps each. Watch the timing. OT-CFM moves every point at a steady pace along almost straight lines — the moons are already visible halfway through. I-CFM first contracts toward the center and then spreads out, so its paths bend. DDIM barely moves for most of the time and does almost all the work in the last few steps, which is exactly why it fails when we give it only one or two steps.
