[Kaiming · ~1.5 min] Is the two-moons result special? Here are all four datasets at a small budget of five steps, with DDPM at its full 1,000 steps as a reference. Rows are moons, eight Gaussians, checkerboard and swiss roll; every method starts from the same noise. OT-CFM at five steps recovers the moons, all eight Gaussian modes and the spiral; only the checkerboard is soft at the edges. I-CFM leaks mass between modes: the eight Gaussians turn into a ring and the moons fill in. DDIM at five steps fails everywhere and collapses into a blob. DDPM gives clean shapes, but needs two hundred times more network calls, and its W2 is still higher than OT-CFM at five steps on all four datasets. The checkerboard is the hardest case for every method: its sharp corners are hard for a small MLP.
