Flow Matching vs. Diffusion · 14

[Kaiming · ~1.5 min] Four experiments, from 2 to 3,072 dimensions. E1: 2D toy data, where we can draw trajectories and densities and also compare with classical estimators, GMM and KDE. Metrics are W2, MMD, likelihood and straightness. E1 is finished for all four datasets with one seed. E2 is the main experiment: MNIST and Fashion-MNIST with one shared UNet, sweeping the number of steps and measuring FID, plus a noise-schedule ablation. E3 evaluates generated data with the course methods — next slides. E4 extends to CIFAR-10 with public weights, compared with published numbers, or tries reflow for one-step generation on MNIST.
