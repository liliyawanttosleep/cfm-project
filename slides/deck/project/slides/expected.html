Flow Matching vs. Diffusion · 23 · Values checked against each paper’s tables

[Wentao · ~1.5 min] What should we expect in high dimensions? Here are published CIFAR-10 numbers, all checked against the original tables. Diffusion: DDPM with 1,000 steps reaches FID 3.17. If you simply cut its stochastic sampler to 10 steps, FID explodes to 367; the deterministic DDIM gets 13.36 at 10 steps and 4.16 at 100. So an ODE sampler is essential for few steps. Flow matching with an adaptive solver, about 130 to 150 network calls: I-CFM 3.66 and OT-CFM 3.58 — almost identical. One-step generation: plain rectified flow with one step gives 378; after one reflow, 12.21; with distillation, 4.85. In high dimensions, few-step generation needs extra work to straighten the flow. Papers differ in networks, training length and FID code, so read trends, not exact gaps; we will measure our own curve in E4.
