Flow Matching vs. Diffusion · 4

[Kaiming · ~1.5 min] The blue line is the diffusion and score line. In 2019 NCSN learned the score with score matching and sampled with Langevin dynamics. In 2020 DDPM turned this into denoising: predict the added noise. In 2021 DDIM made sampling deterministic and fast, and Score SDE unified everything as a stochastic differential equation. EDM in 2022 cleaned up the design space. The orange line is flow matching. In 2023 three papers at almost the same time — Lipman's flow matching, Liu's rectified flow and Albergo's stochastic interpolants — proposed regressing a velocity field directly, without simulating an ODE during training. In 2024 Tong et al. added minibatch optimal-transport pairing, and Stable Diffusion 3 used rectified flow at scale. MeanFlow in 2025 targets one-step generation. Our project compares the blue and the orange lines.
