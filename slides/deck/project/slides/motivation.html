Introduction

Why generative models, and why these two

A statistician’s view: given samples x⁽¹⁾, …, x⁽ⁿ⁾ from an unknown distribution p_data, learn a way to draw new samples from it — in hundreds or thousands of dimensions.

State of the art

Diffusion and flow models generate today’s images, video and audio; Stable Diffusion 3 is a rectified-flow model.

Statistical core

Score matching, regression to a conditional mean, SDEs/ODEs and optimal transport — familiar tools in a new role.

Open question

Under the same network and budget, how do the two families trade sample quality against sampling cost?

Flow Matching vs. Diffusion · 2

【A 讲，约 2 分钟】 1. 统计视角：生成模型就是“从样本学分布，然后能从这个分布里采新样本”，难点在高维（MNIST 784 维、CIFAR 3072 维）。 2. 意义：当前图像、视频、音频生成都是 diffusion / flow 类模型；Stable Diffusion 3（Esser et al. 2024）用的就是 rectified flow。 3. 统计内核：score matching、条件期望回归、SDE/ODE、最优传输——都是统计/概率里熟悉的工具。 4. 我们的问题：同一个网络、同样的训练预算下，两类方法在“生成质量 vs 采样代价（网络调用次数）”上如何取舍？
