Flow Matching vs. Diffusion · 2

[Wentao · ~1.5 min] From a statistician's point of view, a generative model answers one question: given samples from an unknown distribution, how do we draw new samples from it? The hard part is dimension: MNIST has 784 dimensions and CIFAR-10 has 3,072. Diffusion and flow models are the state of the art; Stable Diffusion 3 is a rectified-flow model. Their core is classical statistics: score matching, regression to a conditional mean, SDEs and ODEs, and optimal transport. Our question: with the same network and the same training budget, how do the two families trade sample quality against sampling cost?
