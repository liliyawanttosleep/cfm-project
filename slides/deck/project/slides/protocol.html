[Kaiming · ~1 min] Fairness means only the algorithm changes. Same network, same number of training steps, batch size, optimizer and EMA. All methods sample from the same starting noise and are evaluated on the same held-out test set. Three seeds, mean and standard deviation. We also compute the distance between two independent real samples. That is the noise floor of each metric at our sample size, so we can tell whether a number is actually good. On the right are the settings: the 2D ones are in use; the image training length will be fixed after a pilot run on MNIST.
