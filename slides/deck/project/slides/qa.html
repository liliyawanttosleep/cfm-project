[Both · Q&A] Thank you — we are happy to take questions. Likely questions: 1. What is the essential difference between flow matching and diffusion? The regression target and the probability path; diffusion's ODE sampler (DDIM) is itself a flow, just along a curved path. 2. Why would OT help less in high dimensions? With a batch much smaller than the dimension, empirical OT pairing is close to random pairing (H3). 3. Is FID meaningful on MNIST? Only partly, so we add FID on MNIST-classifier features and the E3 metrics (TSTR, discriminator AUC). 4. Why not GANs or VAEs? We focus on the two families that define the current state of the art; GANs and VAEs appear in the related-work section of the report.
