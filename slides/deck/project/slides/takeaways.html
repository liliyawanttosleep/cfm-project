Flow Matching vs. Diffusion · 25

[Wentao · ~1 min] To summarize. First, diffusion and flow matching are both least-squares regressions of a time-dependent vector field; DDIM is an ODE sampler just like flow matching. Second, what differs is the path: straighter paths, from optimal-transport pairing or reflow, allow far fewer network calls — our 2D experiments already show this clearly. Third, our main open question is how much of that advantage survives in 784 and 3,072 dimensions, which we will answer with MNIST, the course-method evaluation and CIFAR-10.
