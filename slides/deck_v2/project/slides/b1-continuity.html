The marginal field satisfies the continuity equation

1. Mix the conditional paths, with z = (x₀, x₁)

pₜ(x) = E_z[ pₜ(x│z) ]

2. Each conditional path obeys its own equation

∂ₜ pₜ(x│z) + ∇·( uₜ(x│z) pₜ(x│z) ) = 0

3. Average over z, swapping E with ∂ₜ and ∇·

∂ₜ pₜ(x) + ∇·( E_z[ uₜ(x│z) pₜ(x│z) ] ) = 0

4. Name the inside. By Bayes, it is the posterior mean

uₜ(x) pₜ(x) = E_z[ uₜ(x│z) pₜ(x│z) ] ⇒ uₜ(x) = E[ uₜ(x│z) │ xₜ = x ]

Without densities, in one line

With σ = 0, pₜ(x│z) is a point mass. Test against any smooth φ and use the tower rule:

d/dt E φ(xₜ) = E⟨∇φ(xₜ), x₁ − x₀⟩

= E⟨∇φ(xₜ), uₜ(xₜ)⟩

This is the weak form of the continuity equation.

Assumptions

pₜ(x) > 0 where we divide, enough integrability to swap E with ∂ₜ and ∇·, and uₜ regular enough for the ODE to have a unique solution.

Our example

At (t, x) = (0.5, 0.1): weights 0.69 and 0.31, velocities +3.8 and −4.2, so uₜ(x) = 1.32.
