Rectified flow and reflow

Reflow: use the model’s own pairs

1. Train on independent pairs. This first flow is I-CFM with σ = 0.

2. Sample x₀ and solve the ODE to get x̂₁. Keep (x₀, x̂₁).

3. Retrain on these pairs, with the same straight-line regression.

Why it works

Marginal paths of an ODE cannot cross, so the pairs (x₀, x̂₁) are less tangled. Each reflow keeps the marginals and does not raise any convex transport cost (Liu et al. 2023).

Our example

A 1D flow keeps order, so negative noise goes to −2 and positive noise to +2. One reflow on the exact field gives the exact OT pairs of slide 18, and one Euler step is then exact.
