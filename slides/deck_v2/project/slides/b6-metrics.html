Metrics: W2, MMD and FID

W2 (slides 18, 19)

W₂² = min_π E_π‖X − Y‖²

Exact, by assignment on 2,000 samples. Converges like n^(−1/d), so it is useful only in low dimension.

MMD (E1 tables)

MMD² = E k(X, X′) + E k(Y, Y′) − 2 E k(X, Y)

With a sum of RBF kernels. Unbiased estimate, no optimization.

FID (slide 26)

‖μ − μ′‖² + tr( Σ + Σ′ − 2(Σ Σ′)^(1/2) )

W2² between Gaussians fitted to 2,048 Inception features.

FID on MNIST: what to keep in mind

Features

Inception was trained on ImageNet photos, so digits are far from its training domain. We read trends, not absolute values.

Sample size

FID is biased upward at small n. Our real-vs-real reference, 10,000 against all 60,000 training images, shows the floor at this n.

Comparability

Numbers depend on the implementation (clean-fid here), so they do not compare with published CIFAR-10 values.

Variation

Two training seeds per method. They agree at 1–2 steps but not from 10 steps on, so the seed spread is as large as some method gaps.
