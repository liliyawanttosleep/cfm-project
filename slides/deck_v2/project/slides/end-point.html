Flow Matching vs. Diffusion · 3

[Wentao · ~1 min] Before any formula, here is where the method takes us. On the left, points start in eight Gaussian clusters and follow a learned velocity field until they form two moons. On the right, the same idea on images: every pixel starts as noise and flows to a CIFAR-10 image. The left is our own run of the torchcfm tutorial; the right is an example from the library, and we show our own measurements later. The important message is that these two examples use the same loss and the same training loop. Only the data and the network change: a small MLP on the left, a U-Net on the right. Larger image models also use the usual training tools, such as learning-rate warmup and EMA.
