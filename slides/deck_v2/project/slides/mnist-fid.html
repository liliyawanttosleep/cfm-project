Step 4. Quality vs. steps, measured on two seeds

What FID measures

FID = ‖μ − μ′‖² + tr( Σ + Σ′ − 2(Σ Σ′)^(1/2) )

Fit a Gaussian to the Inception features of real images (μ, Σ) and of generated images (μ′, Σ′). Lower is better. Real vs. real: 0.54.

Test 2: holds at 1–2 steps

OT-CFM is lowest at 1–2 steps on both seeds; I-CFM is 1.5–1.7× higher. From 10 steps on, seed 1 reverses.

Test 3: holds at few steps

DDIM is worst from 2 to 20 steps on both seeds. At 100: lowest on seed 0 (12.2), highest on seed 1 (32.8).

Two cautions

At NFE 1 the order of DDIM and I-CFM flips between seeds, so do not read FID at that scale. And our best, 11.5, stays far above the floor: demo-level training.

Flow Matching vs. Diffusion · 26 · 10,000 samples, clean-fid, two training seeds per method. dopri5 takes ~275 calls. ImageNet features: trends only (B6).

[Wentao · ~1.3 min] Now the measurement. FID fits a Gaussian to Inception features of real images and of generated images, then compares their means and covariances. Lower is better, and two sets of real images give 0.54. Inception was trained on ImageNet, so on digits we only read trends. We trained every model twice, with two seeds. Solid lines are seed 0, dashed lines seed 1. Orange is OT-CFM. At one and two steps it is lowest on both seeds: I-CFM is about one and a half times higher. That part of test 2 holds. From 10 steps on, the seeds disagree: on seed 1, I-CFM is lower. DDIM, in blue, is worst from 2 to 20 steps on both seeds, as test 3 predicted. At 100 steps it is the best model on seed 0, 12.2, and the worst on seed 1, 32.8. Same code, same data, only the seed changed. Two cautions. At one step, the order of DDIM and I-CFM flips between seeds, so do not read FID at that scale. And every curve stays far above the floor: demo-level training with no EMA. So OT-CFM helps when steps are very few. How much of the 2D advantage is left?
