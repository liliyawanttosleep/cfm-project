[Wentao · ~1 min] Same data, same network, same starting points; only that one line changed. I-CFM paths curve and loop. OT-CFM paths are almost straight. The table measures this. Path length relative to the straight line falls from 1.35 to 1.001, and straightness from about 10 to 0.02. W2 improves from 0.46 to 0.34, over 100 sampling runs of one trained model each. Remember the warning from step five: the loss falls from about 8 to 0.15 because targets stop conflicting, not because the model is fifty times better. Our small example shows the extreme case. With exact OT pairs, each reachable point has one target, the loss floor goes from 13.69 to zero, and one Euler step is exact. Our old point is not even visited any more. It is not free: training takes about 120 seconds instead of 35 to 61 per 5,000 steps. Does straighter really mean fewer steps?
