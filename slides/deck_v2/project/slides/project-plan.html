Flow Matching vs. Diffusion · 29 · E1: one seed. E2 and E3: two training seeds per method.

[Kaiming · ~1 min] This is where the project stands. E1, the 2D experiment, is done, for one seed. E2 and E3 on MNIST are done for two training seeds per method. The course asks for one course method, and the second table shows why we chose k-means. In a pilot, train on fake and test on real gave about 90 percent for every model, so it could not tell them apart. A real-versus-fake classifier was right almost every time, but mostly because generated backgrounds are not exactly black. PCA with a Fréchet distance worked, but it is one more score like FID. k-means shows which kinds of digits are missing or too frequent. E4 is optional: reflow, aiming at one-step generation. Next: more seeds, since two already disagree, then DDPM with 1,000 steps and Fashion-MNIST.
